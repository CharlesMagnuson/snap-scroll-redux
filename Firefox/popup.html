Snap Scroll Redux

Two powerful scrolling modes:

• Snap Scroll: Hold left + right click

• Next Paragraph: Middle click on any paragraph

Automatically detects and avoids fixed navigation bars

Snap Scroll Mode:

1 Hold down the left mouse button
2 Right-click where you want to scroll to
3 That point will move to the top of the window

Paragraph Navigation:

→ Middle-click on any paragraph to jump to the next one
Blue dot shows when left mouse is held • Works on all websites
